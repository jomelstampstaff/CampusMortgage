About the Bootcamp

An interactive, free study guide for mortgage loan processors — built from real-world training material.

🎯 Purpose

The Certified Master Loan Processor Bootcamp is a self-paced study guide designed to help mortgage loan processors — new and experienced — master the terminology, calculations, documentation, and workflows that drive every loan file.

Every lesson is structured for quick reference: you can jump to a topic, scan the key formulas, review sample computations, and test yourself with practice quizzes.

📚 What's Inside

  • Bootcamp Modules — sequential lessons that build from basics to advanced real-world scenarios.
  • Reference Section — DU/LPA + TRID guide, terminology, formulas cheatsheet, and forms index.
  • Practice Quizzes — every module ends with a quiz to reinforce the material.
  • Interactive Sidebars — collapsible navigation so you can jump to any section instantly.

👥 Who It's For

  • New loan processors starting in the mortgage industry
  • Experienced processors wanting a structured refresher
  • Loan officers and underwriters looking for a shared reference
  • Anyone studying for mortgage industry certifications
