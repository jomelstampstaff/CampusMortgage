1. Credit Report Review Module 2

🔑 First step in every file: Pull the 1003, the credit report, and the photo ID. Get familiar with the borrower — you will see these three items constantly throughout the entire file.

Credit Report Requirements

  • Every file must have a tri-merge credit report for all borrowers
  • Must have at least one national repository credit score for all borrowers
  • Three repositories: TransUnion, Experian, Equifax
  • Credit report must be within 120 days of the note date (180 days for new construction)

Credit Report Review Checklist

1Verify borrower data — name, SSN, address match photo ID and SSN verification
2Review three credit scores — determine mid FICO
3Review all trade lines — mortgages, revolving, installments, leases
4Review public records — judgments, bankruptcies, foreclosures
5Review inquiries — any within 90 days must be evaluated
6Review AKAs and address variations — any within 24 months require LOX
7Review late payments — check overlays (some disallow within 24 months)

Credit Score Determination

Single Borrower Mid FICO = Middle score of the three repositories
Multiple Borrowers Qualifying Score = Lowest of each borrower's middle score
Example (Joint):
Michael: 585, 637, 651 → middle = 637
Megan: 600, 645, 660 → middle = 645
Qualifying score: 637 (lower of the two)

Trade Line Types

Trade LineHow It's Treated
MortgagesFull PITIA counted in DTI
HELOCsUse full line of credit for HCLTV
Installment DebtPayment counted; if <10 payments remaining may be excluded
Revolving DebtPayment counted; utilization considered by AUS
30-Day Charge AccountsCheck guidelines — may need full balance or payment
LeasesAlways counted in DTI (including car leases)
Student LoansCheck guidelines for calculation methods
TimesharesFHA: always included in DTI

Three Letters of Explanation Needed

1. Inquiries

Each inquiry within 90 days. If mortgage-related, must state "shopping for a mortgage, no new debt obtained."

2. Address Variations

Any address within the last 24 months.

3. AKAs

Every AKA on the credit report.

🚨 Red flags: Accounts with payment but no balance (unless closed) · Fraud messages or OFAC hits · SSN discrepancies · Multiple late payments within 24 months · Large inquiries within 90 days

2. Income Review Module 2

🔑 Stable and reliable flow of income is a key consideration in mortgage loan underwriting. If a borrower changes jobs frequently but has consistent and predictable income, they are considered to have a reliable flow of income.

Pay Cycle Reference

Pay CyclePay Periods Per YearPay Stubs for 30 Days
Weekly524 consecutive stubs
Bi-Weekly263 consecutive stubs
Semi-Monthly242 consecutive stubs
Monthly121 stub

Income Documentation Requirements

  • Most recent pay stubs covering 30 days
  • Most recent 2 years W-2s (or per AUS findings)
  • Fully executed written VOE from all jobs in the most recent 2-year period
  • 2 years tax returns when required by AUS or overlays
⚠️ Always do at least two calculations to arrive at the most conservative income. If you submit high income and the underwriter reduces it, you could lose an Approve/Eligible.

3. Base Pay (Salary or Hourly) Module 2

Base pay includes hourly wages, salaries, and average hours worked.

Variable Hours — Declining Trend Use LOWER of: (Current YTD ÷ Months Elapsed) OR ((Current YTD + Previous W-2) ÷ (Months Elapsed + 12))
Variable Hours — Increasing Trend May average Current YTD + Previous Year W-2 income

Base Pay Calculation — Salary

Given: Pay period ends Dec 25; YTD gross $37,440; 2024 W-2 $35,225
Months elapsed: 11 + (25 ÷ 31) = 11 + 0.81 = 11.81 months
Calc 1 — YTD only: $37,440 ÷ 11.81 = $3,170.19/mo
Calc 2 — YTD + Prior W-2: $72,665 ÷ 23.81 = $3,051.87/mo
Use most conservative: $3,051.87/month

Base Pay Calculation — Hourly

Given: $23.50/hr, 32 hrs/wk avg, weekly pay; YTD May 31 = $16,525; 2024 W-2 = $39,500
Calc 1 — Avg Hours × Rate: $23.50 × 32 × 52 = $39,104 ÷ 12 = $3,258.67/mo
Calc 2 — YTD only: $16,525 ÷ 5 = $3,305/mo
Calc 3 — YTD + Prior W-2: $56,025 ÷ 17 = $3,295.59/mo
Use most conservative: $3,258.67/month

4. Bonus, Overtime & Commission Module 2

Requirements

  • Minimum 2-year history required to verify income is stable
  • Completed written VOE breaking out base, OT, and/or commission income
  • Borrower's most recent pay stub and last 2 years W-2s
December 4, 2018 — Fannie Mae Change: Commission income is now treated the same as base income. Tax returns are no longer required just because commission exceeds 25% of income.
🚨 Declining trend: If there is a declining trend in bonus, OT, or commission income, the underwriter may deem the income unacceptable. If you don't need it, don't use it.
24-Month Average (Prior Year Total + Current YTD) ÷ 24 months = Monthly Income
Year-to-Date Only (Most Conservative) Current YTD ÷ Months Elapsed = Monthly Income

Calculation Examples

Overtime Example: Yr 1 = $15,000 · Yr 2 = $12,000 · YTD 6mo = $3,000
YTD only: $3,000 ÷ 6 = $500/mo
Use $500/month (most conservative)
Overtime — substantial decline: Yr 1 = $5,000 · YTD Aug = $1,500
Option A (24-mo avg): $6,500 ÷ 24 = $270.83/mo
Option B (YTD only): $1,500 ÷ 8 = $187.50/mo
Use $187.50/month (most conservative)
Bonus Example: 2024 = $10,000 · 2025 = $5,000 · YTD = $500
12-month conservative: $5,000 ÷ 12 = $416.67/mo
Use $416.67/month (if needed)

5. Part-Time, Second Job & Seasonal Module 2

Part-Time & Second Job

  • Two years of uninterrupted part-time employment
  • Can be combined with history of different employers as long as income consistently received
  • Fully completed written VOE
  • Most recent pay stubs and IRS W-2 forms for most recent 2 years
  • Verbal VOE required from each employer

Seasonal Income

  • Verify borrower worked in the same job or same line of seasonal work for the past 2 years
  • Must confirm with employer there's a reasonable expectation of rehire next season
  • Fully completed VOE, most recent pay stubs, and 2 years W-2s
⚠️ Written VOE for seasonal workers must confirm: Reasonable expectation of rehire + the season they work.

6. Retirement & Pension Income Module 2

Documentation

  • Letters from the organization providing income
  • Retirement award letters
  • Most current signed tax returns
  • IRS 1099 form
  • Proof of current receipt

401(k) Distribution Rules

If paid as a 401(k) distribution:
  • Income must be expected to continue for at least 3 years after application
  • Borrower must have unrestricted access without penalty
Fannie Mae does NOT allow non-employment related assets as a basis for repayment. Freddie Mac DOES allow this.
Freddie Mac Asset Depletion Formula (Asset Balance − Cash to Close) ÷ 240 months = Monthly Qualifying Income
Example: Stock balance $500,000; Cash to close $100,000
Available: $500,000 − $100,000 = $400,000
Monthly: $400,000 ÷ 240 = $1,666.67/mo
Monthly Qualifying Income = $1,666.67

8. Alimony & Child Support Module 2

Documentation Requirements

  • Verify most recent 6 months of receipt
  • At least a 3-year continuance
  • Canceled checks or bank statements
  • Copy of divorce decree or separation agreement
  • Any written legal agreement or court decree
⚠️ Must verify child's date of birth. If support stops at age 18, the child must be young enough for payments to continue for at least 3 years from closing.
To be considered stable: Income must have been received for at least 6 months.

9. Social Security Income Module 2

Types

  • Retirement or long-term disability — no defined expiration date, must be expected to continue
  • Benefits paid to a family member — may be used if 3-year continuance documented
Gross-Up Formula Non-Taxable SS Income × 1.25 = Grossed-Up Amount
To gross up, you MUST have the tax returns. Look at:
  • Line 6A: Total Social Security benefits
  • Line 6B: Taxable portion
Non-taxable portion = 6A − 6B
Year 1: 6A = $21,600 · 6B = $12,137
Non-taxable: $9,463
Grossed up: $9,463 × 1.25 = $11,828.75
Year 2: 6A = $22,500 · 6B = $11,462
Non-taxable: $11,038
Grossed up: $11,038 × 1.25 = $13,797.50
Total grossed up (2 years): $25,626.25
Monthly additional income: $25,626.25 ÷ 24 = $1,067.76/mo
Additional Monthly Income from Gross-Up: $1,067.76

10. Military Income Module 2

Acceptable Income Sources

  • Base pay
  • Flight pay, hazard pay, proficiency pay
  • Rations, clothing allowance, quarters allowance

Military Reservists

  • Income paid while satisfying reserve obligations is acceptable if it satisfies stability and continuity tests for secondary employment
  • Look at military reservists as people who have a second job
⚠️ Base pay is stable UNLESS the borrower is within 12 months of release from active duty. Then request documentation of remaining term of service and ETS.
Quarters Allowance If non-taxable amount can be verified, it CAN be grossed up
🚫 Military education benefits are NOT acceptable income. These funds are allocated for secondary education, not household income.

12. Self-Employment Basics Module 2

A borrower is considered self-employed when they own 25% or more of a company.

Factors to Analyze

  • Stability of the self-employment income
  • Location and nature of the business
  • Demand of the product or services offered
  • Financial strength and ability to continue generating income

Documentation Requirements

  • 1 or 2 years personal and business tax returns (per AUS findings)
  • CPA letter verifying self-employment for last 2 years
  • IRS transcripts matching tax returns
  • Secretary of State (SOS) website for business license info
To waive business returns, ALL must be met:
  • Borrower using own personal funds for cash to close
  • Self-employed in same business for at least 5 years
  • Individual tax returns show an increase in self-employment income over past 2 years

Fannie Mae Form 1084 — Cash Flow Analysis

Provides a written evaluation of a self-employed borrower's personal income. Used to determine stable and continuous income available to the borrower.

Fannie Mae Form 1088 — Comparative Income Analysis

Required when business tax returns are needed. Used to determine business viability by measuring year-to-year trends.

14. Rental Income Module 2

Where Reported

  • Schedule E of borrower's personal tax returns
  • Or partnership/S-corp business returns (Form 8825)

Calculation Rules

  • Add back: depreciation, interest, HOA dues, taxes, insurance
  • Deduct: full PITIA of the property
  • If in service full year: average over 12 months
  • If in service less: average over months used as rental

Forms Used

Form 1037

Single investment property

Form 1038

Multiple properties

Form 1038A

Up to 10 additional properties

Calculation Example

Given from Schedule E:
Total Rents Received: $25,000
Total Expenses: $32,500
Add-backs: Insurance $1,200 + Interest $1,250 + Taxes $5,000
PITIA (from mortgage statement): $1,500
Net: $25,000 − $32,500 + $1,200 + $1,250 + $5,000 = −$50
Monthly: −$50 ÷ 12 = −$4.17/mo
Net Loss: Borrower hit with full PITIA of $1,500/mo

15. Form 1065 — Partnership & K1s Module 2

US Return of Partnership Income. Used to report a borrower's share of partnership income or loss.

Key Items

  • Principal business activity (A, B)
  • Name of partnership, address
  • Business start date — verify 2-year history
  • Line 10: Guaranteed payments to partners
  • Ordinary income/loss from other partnerships (Line 4)
  • Depreciation, depletion, amortization — add back

K1 Form 1065

  • Verify partnership EIN, name, address match Form 1065
  • Verify partner's name and address match borrower
  • Confirm percentage of ownership
  • Key: Box 1 (Ordinary income/loss), Box 2 (Net rental real estate), Box 4 (Guaranteed payments)
Partnership K1 — Qualifying Income Ordinary Income (Box 1) + Net Rental Real Estate (Box 2) + Guaranteed Payments (Box 4) + Depreciation + Depletion + Amortization
⚠️ Multiple partnerships: Request a supporting K1 for EACH one.

16. Form 1120S — S-Corp & K1s Module 2

US Income Tax Return for an S Corporation. More documentation needed than Schedule C.

Key Items

  • Letters A, B: Name, EIN, date incorporated, total assets
  • Ordinary income/loss
  • Other income or loss
  • Depreciation, depletion, amortization — add back
  • Mortgages or notes payable in less than one year — must be SUBTRACTED

K1 Form 1120S

  • Verify corporation EIN, name, address match Form 1120S
  • Verify shareholder name and address match borrower
  • Confirm percentage of stock ownership
  • Key: Box 1 (Ordinary income/loss), Box 2 (Net rental real estate)
S-Corp K1 — Qualifying Income Ordinary Income (Box 1) + Net Rental Real Estate (Box 2) + Depreciation + Depletion + Amortization − Mortgages/Notes Payable ≤ 1 year
🚨 Critical: Mortgages or notes payable in less than one year are SUBTRACTED. Many processors miss this and turn an approved borrower into a denied one.

Form 1065PartnershipYes — each partnership
Form 1120SS-CorpYes — each S-Corp
Form 1120C-Corp (100% owner)No

18. Asset Review Module 2

Funds Required to Close

  • Down payment — purchase price minus new loan
  • Refinance shortfall — payoff minus new loan
  • Closing costs
  • Reserves — required by program after closing

Allowable Assets

Liquid

Checking, savings, money market, CDs

Investment

Stocks, bonds, mutual funds, trust funds

Other

Retirement, bridge loans, gifts, gifts of equity, net equity

Checking & Savings Review

  • Borrower's name on account
  • If another person is on the account (not on loan), may need 100% access letter
  • All pages of statements
  • Verify previous ending balances — if none, request two full months
  • If latest statement is more than 45 days old, request current printout
  • 100% of checking/savings allowed

20. Large Deposits Module 2

Large Deposit Definition Single deposit that exceeds 50% of Total Monthly Qualifying Income

Rules

  • Refinance: Not required to document (but borrowed funds still considered)
  • Purchase: Must be sourced or backed out
  • Only the unsourced portion counts toward the 50% test

Example 1 — Not a Large Deposit

Given: Income $4,000; Deposit $3,000; Documented refund $2,500
Unsourced: $3,000 − $2,500 = $500
$500 ÷ $4,000 = 12.5%
12.5% < 50% — NOT a large deposit
Entire $20,000 balance can be used

Example 2 — IS a Large Deposit

Given: Income $4,000; Deposit $3,000; Documented refund only $500
Unsourced: $3,000 − $500 = $2,500
$2,500 ÷ $4,000 = 62.5%
62.5% > 50% — IS a large deposit
Deduct $2,500 from $20,000
Only $17,500 may be used

21. Stocks, IRA & 401(k) Module 2

Stocks & Mutual Funds

  • Verify current value
  • Most recent statement OR stock certificate + newspaper stock list
  • 100% of value may be used for reserves — liquidation not required
  • If used for cash to close, liquidation IS required

IRA & 401(k)

  • Verify vested funds
  • Acceptable for down payment, closing costs, reserves
  • Verify ownership and withdrawal availability
  • If using for cash to close: provide terms of withdrawal
  • If using for reserves only: vested balance minus outstanding loans
⚠️ Check overlays for any requirement to hit for taxes or penalties.

26. Title Review Module 2

An agreement between insurer and lender providing protection from loss due to defects in the title.

Title Commitment Components

  • Title Certification
  • Schedule A (current status)
  • Schedules B1, B2 (requirements and exceptions)
  • Judgment, bankruptcy, mortgage, Patriot searches
  • Tax and water lien searches

Potential Title Defects

  • Forged deeds or other legal documents
  • Failure of spouses to join conveyances
  • Undisclosed or missing heirs
  • Deeds from minors or those of unsound mind
  • Errors in indexing public records
  • Liens for unpaid taxes
  • Erroneous reports from tax officials
  • Deeds from defunct corporations or unprobated wills

Title Review Checklist

  • Effective date within 60–90 days of closing
  • Record title ID number kept handy
  • Policy amounts match first and second liens
  • Owner of record must match: sales contract, title, appraisal
  • Legal description must match across all three
  • Schedule C — verify only expected liens on refinance
Must Match: Sales contract ↔ Title commitment ↔ Appraisal

27. Possible Underwriting Conditions Module 2

🔑 Every underwriter underwrites differently. Conditions may vary between underwriters and lenders. But agency requirements must be consistently met.

Common Conditions

  • Credit supplement for mortgage accounts paid 0×30 in last 12 months
  • Evidence of student loan repayment terms (or 1% of balance)
  • Signed LOXs for AKAs, address variations, inquiries
  • LOX for purchasing another primary residence
  • Flood insurance if in flood zone
  • Legible front/back of permanent resident alien card
  • Signed and dated initial 1003
  • Arms-length affidavit for FSBO
  • Evidence current mortgage paid current
  • Power of Attorney executed prior to CTC
  • 12 months VOR or VOM
  • Credit explanation for derogatory items
  • Signed rent-free letter
  • Verbal VOE for all jobs in last 2 years
  • CPA letter, business license, or articles of incorporation
  • Fully executed sales contract with addendums
  • HOA questionnaire for condo project approval
  • Short sale approval
  • 30 days pay stubs, 2 years W-2s
  • 2 years 1040s with all schedules
  • Fully executed CD or settlement statement from sale of current residence
  • VOD or 2 months bank statements
  • Earnest money deposit and source of funds
