DU & LPA: Foundations Reference

What are DU and LPA?

SystemFull NameOwnerUsed By
DUDesktop UnderwriterFannie MaeLenders
DODesktop OriginatorFannie MaeBrokers / TPOs
LPALoan Product AdvisorFreddie MacLenders
🔑 Both are tools, not replacements for human underwriters. Neither evaluates compliance with federal/state laws.

Scopes of Analysis

💳 Credit Risk Factors
  • Credit history & delinquencies
  • Installment & mortgage accounts
  • Foreclosures, bankruptcies
  • Revolving credit utilization
  • Collections, inquiries
🏠 Non-Credit Risk Factors
  • LTV, CLTV, HLTV
  • Liquid reserves
  • Loan purpose, terms, amortization
  • Occupancy, ratios, property type
  • Co-borrowers, self-employment

Reps & Warrants

A set of assurances lenders make to the GSE regarding loan quality. Relief is provided when information is validated, all messaging is complied with, and final recommendation matches final documents.

Relief voided if lender fails to validate employment or misrepresents data. Lender must buy back the loan.

Data Integrity — "Garbage in, garbage out"

Critical fields to verify:

  • Borrower name / DOB / SSN
  • Loan terms (update when term changes)
  • LTV / CLTV / HLTV (include subordinate financing)
  • Sales price, appraised value
  • Income amounts & sources
  • DTI ratios
  • Asset amounts & sources
  • Property / occupancy type

DU Red Flags & Tolerances Reference

Red Flag Messages

🚩 Excessive Resubmissions

50-60 runs indicates possible manipulation. After 19 runs, flag stays permanently.

🚩 Frozen Credit Report

One or more bureaus frozen. If one frozen, proceed if data from 2 repositories available.

🚩 Potential Case File ID Reuse

Critical data changed. Investigate; open new DU if second loan.

🚩 Occupancy Modified

Investment changed to owner-occupied. Investigate with due diligence.

DU Recommendations

RecommendationMeaning
Approve EligibleMeets credit risk AND loan eligibility
Approve IneligibleMeets credit risk but not eligibility
Refer with CautionDoes not meet credit or eligibility
Out of ScopeUnable to underwrite as submitted

DTI Tolerances

Original → NewResubmit?Reason
35% → 40%✅ YesExceeds 3% tolerance
44% → 46%✅ YesGoes over 45%
46% → 48%❌ NoOnly 2% increase; already >45%
48% → 51%✅ YesExceeds 50% max

Other Tolerances

  • Rate increase: Always resubmit
  • Rate decrease (not from buyown): No resubmit
  • Funds to close exceed findings: Document sufficient assets; no resubmit
  • Reserves less than required: Verify ≥90% of required; no resubmit
  • Refinance loan amount: May increase $500 or 1% (whichever less); may decrease 5%

RecommendationMeaning

CautionUnlikely to meet standards; excessive layered risk
Streamline AcceptAllows reduced documentation

Ineligible/Incomplete/InvalidUnable to underwrite as submitted

LPA Tolerances (No Resubmission)

  • Monthly debt payment decreases
  • Income for any borrower increases
  • Income decreases if DTI doesn't increase >3% or go over 45%
  • Debt increases if DTI doesn't increase >3% or go over 45%
  • Verified assets/reserves increase
  • Verified reserves decrease by no less than amount required
  • Loan amount decreases ≤5% on refinance (if MI not impacted)

Total Scorecard (FHA/VA) Reference

TOTAL = Technology Open to Approved Lenders. An overlay accessed through DU and LPA — not a standalone AUS.

RecommendationMeaning
Accept EligibleMay be eligible for insurance; docs must be validated
ReferMust be underwritten by a DE underwriter
IneligibleMay be other ineligible characteristics
FHA: Accept eligible means FHA will insure without manual underwriting unless manual downgrade needed.

Lender Responsibilities Reference

  • Confirm accuracy of data — file must match AUS exactly
  • Employ prudent underwriting judgment
  • Comply with all verification messages
  • Exercise due diligence when reviewing documentation
  • Ensure derogatory/contradictory info is part of findings
  • Take action when errors not referenced — rerun AUS
  • Review credit reports — trade lines current, student loans reflected
  • Update LOS and AUS with every change

Non-Traditional Credit Reference

DU Requirements (No Score)
  • Primary residence, all occupy
  • One unit, purchase/limited cash-out
  • Fixed-rate, conforming limits
  • Max LTV/CLTV/HLTV: 90%
  • DTI < 40%
LPA Requirements (No Score)
  • Purchase or no cash-out refi
  • One-unit, all occupy as primary
  • Fixed-rate
  • Max LTV/TLTV/HTLTV: 95%
  • Standard conforming limits
Document two non-traditional credit sources per borrower — each with 12-month pay history; one must be housing-related.

Validation Services (D1C & AIM) Reference

Fannie Mae: Day One Certainty (D1C)

Uses third-party vendor reports for VOE, VOI, VOA. If validated, no further documentation required.

Freddie Mac: Asset & Income Modeler (AIM)

Same concept — integrates electronic data from approved vendors.

What Can Be Validated

  • Employment (via The Work Number, etc.)
  • Income (bonus, OT, commission)
  • Assets (60 days transaction history)
  • Tax transcripts (self-employment, retirement, SS)

What Cannot Be Validated

  • Military service members
  • Child support or alimony
  • Self-employed employment (income may be)
  • Self-employed income on Form 1065 or 1120S
  • Gifts or business assets for self-employed borrower
Documentation expiration: Same as any other — not over 120 days old at note execution. Exception: VOE must be updated within 10 calendar days of closing.

UCDP & Appraisals Reference

Electronic delivery system for appraisals to Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac. For lenders only — AMCs submit as agent.

Key Facts

  • Conventional loans requiring appraisal
  • All appraisals must be uploaded: single family, manufactured, 2-4 units, condos, co-ops, exterior-only, repairs (1004D/442)
  • Maintains up to 3 appraisals per loan
  • Field reviews are not submitted
  • Must receive Successful SSR status before delivery

SSR Message Types

🔴 Hard Stop

Must be cleared

🟡 Warning / Overridable

Manually overwrite or upload corrected appraisal

⚫ Fatal

Missing data; obtain corrected appraisal and resubmit

Collateral Underwriter (CU) & ACE Reference

Fannie Mae: Collateral Underwriter
  • Risk score 1.0 (low) to 5.0 (high)
  • Available on one-unit attached/detached condos (not manufactured)
  • Risk score ≤2.5 for relief
  • Still need Form 442 on required repairs
Freddie Mac: ACE
  • LPA must provide Accept risk class
  • Single-family one-unit (incl. condos)
  • Primary or second home
  • Purchase/No cash-out: Max LTV ≤90%
  • Cash-out (primary): ≤70%; (second): ≤60%

When Appraisal Waiver Cannot Be Used (DU)

  • Lender has knowledge appraisal is warranted
  • Proposed construction / construction-to-perm
  • 2-4 units when value ≥$1 million
  • HomeStyle, Texas Section 50(a)(6)
  • Leaseholds, resale restrictions, co-ops, manufactured homes
  • Gift of equity
  • Major repairs required affecting safety/soundness
  • Borrower requests appraisal
Due diligence with waiver: FEMA disaster area — verify no damage. Home inspection — confirm not C6. Refinance — certify not listed for sale (cash-out not acquired in last 6 months).

TRID: Purpose Reference

TRID (TILA‑RESPA Integrated Disclosure) is a CFPB tool to ensure lenders follow the Dodd‑Frank "Know Before You Owe" rule. It protects and informs consumers by simplifying the loan process.

🔑 Applies to closed‑end loans only. Not reverse mortgages, HELOCs, mobile homes without land.

Before vs. After

📄 Before (4 documents)
  • Good Faith Estimate (GFE)
  • Initial Truth in Lending (TIL)
  • HUD‑1 Settlement Statement
  • Final TIL
📄 After (2 documents)
  • Loan Estimate (LE) – GFE + initial TIL
  • Closing Disclosure (CD) – HUD‑1 + final TIL

Application Trigger

The loan application is triggered when six pieces are collected. LE must be delivered within 3 business days.

1Full name
2Social Security Number
3Income
4Property address
5Estimated value
6Desired loan amount

TRID: Forms & Acronyms Reference

📌 LE

Loan Estimate — within 3 business days of application.

📌 CD

Closing Disclosure — received 3 business days before consummation.

📌 Acronyms

APR, TILA, RESPA, CFPB, LE, CD, TRID, NMLS, MLO

New Data Points

  • Realtor's license number
  • Services borrower can/cannot shop for
  • Payment comparison over loan term
  • Lender's responsibility for appraisal copy
  • Rate lock confirmation
  • Contact info + NMLS IDs
🚫 Pre‑disclosure restriction: You cannot create a "fake" LE‑looking form for pre‑qualification.

TRID: Loan Estimate Reference

Delivered within 3 business days of application. Shows accurate terms and costs.

SectionIncludes
Loan TermsAmount, rate, monthly P&I, prepayment penalty, MI & payment changes
Closing Cost DetailsA. Origination · B. Cannot Shop · C. Can Shop · E. Taxes · F. Prepaids · G. Escrow
Cash to CloseDown payment, deposits, seller credits, adjustments
Other ConsiderationsAppraisal, assumption, insurance, late payment, refinance, servicing

📬 Delivery: If not electronic (with acknowledgment), must be mailed within 3 business days.
⏳ Intent to Proceed: Required before collecting any fees (except credit report).

TRID: Closing Disclosure Reference

Must be received by borrower at least 3 business days before consummation.

Key Additions

  • Settlement agent, file number, seller info, MIC number
  • LE vs. final cost comparison (line‑item)
  • "Paid by others" column (including lender credits)
  • Recording fees lumped together
  • Owner's title policy disclosure
  • APR, finance charge, TIP
  • Escrow account details / waiver
  • NMLS ID placement (must be on correct line)
✍️ Delivery: Electronic or mail. If mail, place in mail no later than 3 business days before consummation. Business day for CD = all calendar days except Sundays and legal holidays (Saturdays count).

Who Provides the CD?

  • Settlement agent must provide to seller.
  • Lender can contract settlement agent to provide to buyer — usually lender retains responsibility.
  • Cash transactions: CD not required, but state laws may require a form.

TRID: Timing Reference

📅 Loan Estimate
  • Within 3 business days of application
  • Business day = creditor open for substantially all business (Sat. excluded)
  • If electronic not acknowledged by day 3, must mail
  • Intent to Proceed required before fees (except credit report)
📅 Closing Disclosure
  • Received at least 3 business days before consummation
  • Business day = all calendar days except Sundays & legal holidays (Sat. counts)
  • Mail: place in mail no later than 3 business days before closing
  • Redisclosure triggers new 3‑day waiting period
📬 Electronic delivery: Allowed, but must follow E‑SIGN and privacy rules. Acknowledgment of receipt is critical.

TRID: Fee Tolerance Limits Reference

🔴 Zero Tolerance

Cannot increase unless valid change of circumstance.

  • Lender/broker fees
  • Transfer taxes
  • FHA/VA upfront premiums (if disclosed incorrectly)
  • Services borrower cannot shop for
🟡 10% Cumulative

Total cannot increase more than 10%.

  • Recording fees
  • Services borrower can shop for

Sum all fees; if total >10%, lender pays difference.

🟢 No Limit / Good Faith

Can increase freely if good faith and changed circumstances.

  • Prepaids (insurance, interest)
  • Escrow deposits
  • Property taxes
🚨 Human error is not a change of circumstance. Underestimating FHA premiums, transfer taxes, or lender fees — lender absorbs the cost.

TRID: Redisclosing & Change of Circumstance Reference

A valid change of circumstance allows redisclosure of the LE (and possibly new CD waiting period).

✅ Valid
  • Rate lock (points/fees change)
  • Bona fide discount point buy‑down
  • Appraisal different than expected
  • Title issues (e.g., quitclaim deed)
  • Natural disaster, job loss
  • Loan product change (documented)
❌ Not Valid
  • Human error / underestimating costs
  • Adding forgotten lender fees
  • Changing origination fees without valid reason
📝 Document everything: Keep letters, system logs, and explanations. Auditors will ask for proof.
