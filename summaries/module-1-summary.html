Loan Processing 101 — The Basics

The essentials in about 10 minutes. For the full deep dive, use the "Go Deeper" link at the bottom.

⚡ In 60 Seconds

Loan Processing 101 covers the foundation of mortgage processing — the terminology you'll hear every day, the three key ratios (LTV, CLTV, HCLTV) that define how much a borrower can finance, how the loan cycle flows from application to closing, what documents are required at each stage, and the key forms (1003, 1008, LE, CD) that drive every transaction. It also introduces TRID disclosure rules and the three disclosure documents that must go out within 3 business days of application.

⭐ The 10 Things You Must Remember

  1. LTV, CLTV, HCLTV use the lesser of appraised value or purchase price.
  2. HELOC = full line limit, not the balance, for HCLTV calculation.
  3. PITIA includes HOA dues; PITI does not. Always qualify on PITIA if there's an HOA.
  4. DTI has two sides: front-end (housing) and back-end (all monthly debt).
  5. Escrow can only be waived if LTV ≤ 80% (20% down).
  6. Document expiration: 90–120 days standard; 180 days for new construction.
  7. Three disclosures within 3 business days of application: LE, Servicing, ECOA.
  8. CD must be delivered at least 3 business days before consummation.
  9. Six items trigger an application: name, SSN, income, property address, estimated value, loan amount.
  10. Maximum 4 borrowers supported by DU/LPA. 5+ requires manual underwrite.

📐 Cheatsheet — Critical Formulas

LTV (Loan-to-Value)

LTV First Mortgage Loan Amount ÷ Lesser of Appraised Value or Purchase Price

Round up to nearest whole %.

CLTV (Combined Loan-to-Value)

CLTV (First Loan + All Subordinate Liens) ÷ Lesser of Appraised Value or Purchase Price

HCLTV (High Combined Loan-to-Value)

HCLTV (First Loan + Total HELOC Credit Limit) ÷ Lesser of Appraised Value or Purchase Price

Front-End DTI

Front-End Ratio PITIA ÷ Total Qualifying Income

Back-End DTI

Back-End Ratio (PITIA + All Other Monthly Debts) ÷ Total Qualifying Income

Cash to Close (Purchase)

Purchase Cash to Close (Sales Price − Loan Amount) + Closing Costs = Cash to Close

Cash to Close (Refinance)

Refinance Cash to Close (Payoff − New Loan) + Closing Costs = Cash to Close

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Using the balance instead of the full HELOC line limit for HCLTV.
  • Forgetting HOA dues in the DTI calculation (PITIA, not PITI).
  • Counting the same day when calculating the 3-day CD window.
  • Mixing up LE business day (specific) vs. CD business day (general).
  • Missing that some fees have 0% tolerance on the LE.
  • Not verifying document expiration dates before each AUS run.
  • Sending initial disclosures after the 3-business-day window.
  • Forgetting that maximum 4 borrowers can be processed through DU/LPA.
