⭐ The 10 Things You Must Remember

  1. Always do at least 2 income calculations and use the most conservative number for qualifying.
  2. Bonus, OT, and commission require 2-year history. If declining, either don't use it or use the lower amount.
  3. For self-employed: add back depreciation, depletion, amortization — and subtract mortgages/notes payable ≤ 1 year on 1120S.
  4. Rental income: add back expenses, deduct the full PITIA of the rental property.
  5. Social Security gross-up: non-taxable portion × 1.25. Must have tax returns to calculate.
  6. Large deposit = single deposit > 50% of monthly qualifying income. Source it or back it out.
  7. Gift funds: no cousins. Need gift letter + evidence of donor's funds + proof of transfer.
  8. Sales contract, appraisal, and title must all match on: property address, owner of record, and legal description.
  9. Every self-employed file needs: 1084 (cash flow analysis) and 1088 (comparative income analysis).
  10. Processor certification reduces underwriter conditions — always include a memo for any unclear items.

📐 Cheatsheet — Critical Formulas

Variable Hours (Declining Trend)

Most Conservative Use LOWER of: (Current YTD ÷ Months Elapsed) OR ((Current YTD + Prior W-2) ÷ (Months Elapsed + 12))

Base Pay Calculation

Formula (Current YTD + Prior Year W-2) ÷ (Months Elapsed + 12) = Monthly Income

Bonus/OT/Commission — Declining

Formula Use LOWER of: (Current YTD ÷ Months Elapsed) OR ((Prior Year + YTD) ÷ 24 months)

Social Security Gross-Up

Formula Non-Taxable SS Income × 1.25 = Grossed-Up Amount

Freddie Mac Asset Depletion

Formula (Asset Balance − Cash to Close) ÷ 240 months = Monthly Qualifying Income

Large Deposit Test

Rule Single Deposit > 50% of Monthly Qualifying Income = Large Deposit

Schedule C Qualifying Income

Formula Net Profit + Depreciation + Business Use of Home + Amortization + Casualty Loss − Non-Deductible Travel/Meals

Partnership / S-Corp K1 Qualifying Income

Formula Ordinary Income (Box 1) + Net Rental (Box 2) + Guaranteed Payments + Depreciation + Depletion + Amortization − Notes Payable ≤ 1 Year

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Submitting high income instead of the most conservative — underwriter will reduce it and you'll lose the approval.
  • Missing mortgages or notes payable ≤ 1 year on an 1120S — this reduces qualifying income.
  • Using the balance instead of the full HELOC line for HCLTV calculation.
  • Not checking depreciation add-backs on Schedule C — you're leaving income on the table.
  • Forgetting that cousins are NOT eligible gift donors.
  • Missing large deposits in bank statements — 50% threshold rule.
  • Not requesting K-1s when 1065 or 1120S returns are in the file.
  • Using 1% of balance for student loan on FHA (correct) vs. 0.5% on VA — mixing them up.
  • Relying on the appraisal to match tax amounts — use the actual tax cert from title instead.
  • Letting the file reach the underwriter without a processor memo covering red flags.
